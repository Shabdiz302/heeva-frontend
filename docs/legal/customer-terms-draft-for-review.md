## 1. Who we are
Heeva Courses Ltd ("Heeva", "we", "us") operates an online education platform featuring courses and educators for children and adults and professional development services for organisations. Our listed address is 124 City Road, London, England, EC1V 2NX; contact info@heevacourses.com. **VERIFY registered details** before release.

The contractual role of Heeva and any independent educator must be clearly identified at the point of purchase. We will show whether a lesson is supplied by Heeva or by an independent educator through the marketplace. We must not call Heeva merely an intermediary if it actually sells the lesson to the consumer as principal.

## 2. What these terms cover
These terms would apply to customer access to the website, learner or guardian accounts, enquiries, course requests, lessons and paid course packages. Additional course-specific terms, prominently shown before ordering, would identify schedule, format, educator, fees and cancellation arrangements. B2B consultancy must have a separate written engagement agreement; these consumer terms are not a substitute for that agreement.

## 3. Accounts and minors
A parent or lawful guardian must create and manage any account for a child under the applicable minimum age, provide necessary permissions, keep account credentials secure and tell Heeva about unauthorised access. Children must not be directed to enter unnecessary sensitive personal information. Only information needed for a safe learning service should be collected.

We may suspend accounts for serious misuse or safeguarding concerns, subject to applicable consumer rights and a fair process. Suspension does not automatically remove statutory refund rights.

## 4. Course information, suitability and availability
Each listing should identify whether the course is currently available or **Coming soon**, the level and intended learners, lesson duration, format, package information (if relevant) and the steps required to enrol. A proposed course is not an offer of a confirmed booking. Educator profiles must not be represented as confirmation of tutor availability, accreditation or a guaranteed outcome.

## 5. Enquiries and booking confirmation
An enquiry, expression of interest or request for an introductory lesson is **not** a confirmed booking. Heeva will confirm the educator, date/time, timezone, lesson format and any price before a contract is formed. The actual checkout must explicitly explain when a customer commits to paying. An acknowledgement of receipt is not necessarily acceptance of an order unless the confirmation says so.

## 6. One free introductory lesson
Heeva's intended offer is **one free introductory lesson per eligible learner**, not two and not a free lesson with every tutor or course. Eligibility, available subjects, scheduling, missed sessions and any limitations must be disclosed transparently before a customer requests it. The trial system must prevent duplicate redemption by the same learner without unfairly treating multiple children in one family as one learner. **Final eligibility conditions still require approval.**

No payment or ongoing package purchase should be triggered merely by requesting a free introductory lesson.

## 7. Prices and payments
The price, duration, session count, applicable taxes and any additional charges will be displayed and confirmed before payment. We will not charge for unconfirmed packages or add costs without the customer's express agreement. Payments would be handled by a configured regulated payment service provider; Heeva should not store raw card numbers. **Provider, refund destination, taxes and invoice policy to be completed.**

## 8. Scheduling, changes and missed lessons
Heeva and the tutor will communicate confirmed lesson times, access instructions and changes. If Heeva or an educator cancels a confirmed paid lesson, we will offer a reasonable reschedule or an appropriate remedy, including a refund where legally required. If a learner wishes to cancel or reschedule, the applicable notice requirements and consequences will be prominently stated at booking. **Do not fill this clause with an arbitrary 24/48-hour cancellation penalty without approving fairness and the actual operating process.**

## 9. Cancellations, refunds and consumer rights
Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer protection law, including services performed with reasonable care and skill and statutory cancellation rights where applicable.

For eligible UK distance contracts, customers may have a statutory 14-day cancellation period, subject to the legal rules and exceptions applicable to the particular service. If services are to begin during that period, Heeva must obtain and record the required express request/acknowledgement and explain any proportionate charges or circumstances in which cancellation rights are lost. Do not automatically treat all online tutoring services as digital content; live teaching is a service and may be subject to different cancellation treatment. **UK legal counsel must review exceptions and early-start checkout consent.**

A complete operational refund policy must explain prepaid unused sessions, tutor cancellations, faulty service, withdrawals, partial packages, refunds and processing time before launch.

## 10. Learning experience and responsibilities
Learners and guardians should attend on time, use the agreed video platform responsibly and behave respectfully towards educators and other participants. Educators should deliver agreed lessons with reasonable care and skill. Technical issues, access failures and lost sessions should be managed fairly, including situations where the problem originates with Heeva or the educator.

## 11. Safeguarding and privacy
Heeva is committed to safe learning arrangements. Guardians should review the published safeguarding and online conduct policies, which must identify reporting contacts and procedures. Children should not contact tutors outside approved channels or share unnecessary personal information. The actual recording policy, supervision arrangements and safeguarding process must be specified and supported operationally before launch.

Personal information is handled under the applicable Privacy Policy, which must clearly explain lawful bases, retention, processors and children’s privacy controls. Consent for marketing and AI must be separate from agreement to essential service terms where required.

## 12. Intellectual property and teaching materials
Materials made available for a course are for the enrolled learner's personal use unless a separate licence says otherwise. Tutors must own or be licensed to supply what they upload. Nothing in this section should prevent lawful uses or consumer rights. **Clarify treatment of student-created works and recordings.**

## 13. Complaints and contact
For enquiries, cancellation requests, complaints or safeguarding concerns, contact info@heevacourses.com. Heeva should provide an accessible complaints process, response milestones and an urgent child-safety escalation route. **Add verified safeguarding contact information before launch.**

## 14. Responsibility and fairness
Nothing limits liability for fraud, death or personal injury caused by negligence, or any other liability that law prohibits us from restricting. We will not insert blanket disclaimers of responsibility for educator acts where those are inconsistent with Heeva's actual contractual role or customer statutory rights. A lawyer must review proportionate liability wording.

## 15. Changes, legal rights and contact
Material changes to an agreed paid booking should not be applied retrospectively without a lawful basis and appropriate customer remedies. The governing-law and dispute provisions must preserve any mandatory consumer rights in the customer's country of residence. **Complete and review governing law/jurisdiction wording based on actual service markets.**

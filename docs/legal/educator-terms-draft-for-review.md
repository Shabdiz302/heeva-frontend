Prepared: 21 November 2026. Terms for an educator joining a platform offering lessons and/or course programmes to consumers. This is separate from consumer Terms and Conditions and must be approved before educator onboarding becomes available publicly.

## 1. Applying to Heeva
Educators register for a private account, verify their email, complete a profile, identify subjects, supply evidence where requested and propose lesson rates and courses. Submission is an application, not guaranteed acceptance or an offer of paid teaching work.

Heeva may request clarifications, verify information, reject incomplete applications on objective grounds or suspend publication while safeguarding concerns are investigated. Review criteria should be fair, documented and administered consistently.

## 2. Verification, accuracy and safeguarding
The educator must provide truthful information about identity, qualifications, experience, skills, teaching eligibility and applicable checks; keep it current; and cooperate with reasonable verification requests. The educator must not claim a DBS check or professional accreditation that was not verified, or teach children under arrangements that Heeva has not authorised.

Heeva must independently verify relevant identity and safeguarding requirements before publishing a child-facing profile or arranging lessons; a checkbox alone cannot substitute for this process.

## 3. Profile creation, AI tools and media
Educators may prepare biographies, profile photographs and authentic introduction videos in their private workspace. Optional AI tools may suggest factual wording, lighting correction, cropping and captions; educators must check their output. No artificial qualifications, identity changes, synthetic likeness or made-up teaching demonstrations may be published.

Heeva reviews a submitted profile and creates a separate public snapshot after approval. Subsequent changes remain drafts until reapproved. Heeva may unpublish swiftly for safeguarding or substantiated compliance reasons. Educators remain responsible for having permission to upload their media and teaching materials; permissions and processing details must be disclosed in the Privacy Policy.

## 4. Course proposals and prices
Educators may submit private proposed course descriptions, lesson durations, learner suitability, schedules and proposed compensation. Heeva must approve any customer-facing course offer and the final consumer price before publication. Approval of an educator is **not** blanket approval of every course or rate.

All price displays must explain the learner price, educator earnings and any commission or fees separately where relevant. **The exact formula and payment terms remain to be commercially agreed.**

## 5. Lessons and professional conduct
Educators agree to prepare, attend and teach agreed sessions with reasonable care and professional skill, maintain clear communication and follow Heeva's safeguarding and attendance policies. No unsupervised or unauthorised contact with child learners, inappropriate sharing of identifying details or requests for payments outside authorised channels.

Lesson cancellations, absences, technical failures and make-up lessons are handled according to the agreed operating policy, which must balance learners' and educators' legitimate interests.

## 6. Payments, refunds and disputes
An educator is paid only according to the individually accepted commercial schedule (after legal deductions where applicable) and any agreed reconciliations. The contract must describe payout timing, cancellations, chargebacks, refunds, taxes and supporting statements. Heeva should not withhold undisputed earned amounts arbitrarily. **Do not activate payouts without the actual payment processor, tax analysis and agreed terms.**

## 7. Privacy and confidentiality
Educators may access only learner information required for assigned teaching. Learner data, especially information relating to children, must not be copied into AI tools, personal marketing lists, or insecure third-party services. Report suspected data breaches and safeguarding concerns promptly through official Heeva channels.

## 8. Original materials and marketing permission
The educator must have the rights required to teach from and upload their content. Any licence to Heeva for displaying an approved public profile or promoting a course should be non-exclusive, limited to legitimate platform activities and end when appropriate, subject to reasonable archival records. Detailed rights and takedown rules must be agreed, including how learner-generated work is handled.

## 9. Status, taxes, and insurance
The agreement will state how educators are engaged, but any self-employment classification must reflect the real relationship under applicable law. Tax, insurance and work eligibility responsibilities must be allocated accurately and not contradict statutory rights.

## 10. Suspension, termination and disputes
Define a fair notice process, reasonable ability to correct issues, urgent temporary suspension for child safety or serious misconduct, learner continuity, handover, retention/deletion and payment of outstanding valid earnings. No indefinite forfeiture of earned fees.

## 11. Contract updates and law
Material changes to educator commissions and obligations should be notified and accepted in a legally effective way. Local mandatory laws must be respected.
